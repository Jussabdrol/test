GOOD TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Who is Bop for?

Bop is for organizations that want to connect planning, risk management and compliance. It helps quality managers, operational teams and leadership turn policies into everyday practice.

What happens after I purchase?

Once the payment provider confirms your payment, Bop activates your organization and administrator account. The confirmation page tells you when your workspace is ready. Sign in with the email and password you chose during checkout.

Can my team use Bop too?

Yes. Your license covers one organization. As an administrator, you can invite colleagues and manage their roles and module access. This license has no per-user charge.

Does Bop make my organization compliant?

Bop helps you organize policies, controls, assessments and evidence. Your organization remains responsible for their content, execution and evaluation. Using Bop does not constitute certification.

How do renewals and cancellation work?

Your license renews monthly or annually. Once billing is available, you can manage your subscription and invoices from your account. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your access continues until the end of your paid period.

What about AI features?

Bop includes AI governance features and an AI assistant. Assistant availability depends on configuration and usage limits. The license does not include unlimited AI usage.
