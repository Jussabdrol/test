GOOD TO KNOW

A few clear answers.

Can I buy Bop or create an account today?

New self-service subscriptions are not open yet. Explore the product tour and guides now. Existing users can sign in to their workspace. No payment is collected on this website.

Do I need a sales call or onboarding session?

No sales call is needed to explore Bop. The planned subscription is designed for teams that set up and run their own workspace using the guides.

Does Bop get us certified?

Bop helps organize your management system. Your team remains responsible for its scope, content, implementation and evaluation. Certification is a separate process with an independent certification body.

What does support include?

Start with the public guides. For a software issue or product question, use the contact form to open a ticket. Support is in writing, without a live-chat or 24/7 response commitment. Consulting, custom development and done-for-you implementation are outside the plan.

Can my whole team use it?

The planned license covers one organization without per-user charges. Administrators manage user roles and module permissions. Separate organizations need separate licenses.

Are AI usage and integrations unlimited?

No. The AI assistant depends on configuration and usage limits. Bop is not a promise of unlimited AI, automated evidence collection from every system or custom integrations. Review your needs before choosing a subscription.
