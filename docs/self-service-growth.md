# Bop: manageable self-service growth

Owner constraint: a solo business alongside work and family. Optimize for suitable
customers and low support per organization, rather than maximum lead volume.
The first audience is an English-speaking small team with an existing ISO 27001
or ISO 9001 system and an internal process owner. Broader compliance claims create
more expectations than the current product should promise.

## Conversion path implemented

Search or referral → clear homepage → four-step product illustration → transparent
plan and scope → availability page. Guides answer setup questions without an
account. The contact form asks for enough detail to handle a software question in
writing and routes it into the platform administrator's queue. Sales calls, paid
setup, custom proposals and consulting retainers are outside this offer.

Online checkout intentionally remains closed until Stripe and automated onboarding
are ready. Do not fill this gap with manual account creation as the advertised
sales process: that makes every conversion a founder task.

## Traffic without a daily content obligation

1. Build on the searchable guides already published. Improve one useful page per
   month from recurring questions: recurring ISO controls, evidence per execution,
   access reviews and audit follow-up. Show actual workflows and label sample data.
2. Once signup works, add focused landing pages for ISO 27001 small teams and ISO
   9001 recurring checks, with distinct examples and honest scope. Avoid hundreds
   of near-identical generated pages and unverified competitor comparisons.
3. Make each guide easy for existing customers to share with their team or adviser.
   Later, test a simple referral/partner link that goes to the self-service path;
   avoid a partner programme requiring custom demos and individual contracts.
4. Verify the domain in a search console and submit `/sitemap.xml` using the owner's
   existing account when available. This release adds the files but does not claim
   search-console submission, rankings, analytics or campaign activation.
5. Only after checkout/onboarding validation, run one small, capped intent-search
   experiment. Set the budget explicitly first. Do not buy broad “compliance” clicks
   while visitors cannot subscribe. No advertising spend is authorized or incurred.

## A bounded operating routine

Suggested initial routine, to adjust after real usage: two scheduled queue reviews
per week, plus a defined way to notice genuine service/security incidents. This is
an internal planning assumption, not a public support SLA. The current release does
not send ticket notifications or automate these checks.

Resolve product questions with a link to a relevant guide and a short specific
answer. When the same question occurs three times, improve the guide or interface.
Keep consulting and custom development outside the plan. Record time spent per
ticket and per paying organization; if support grows faster than revenue, fix the
onboarding/product issue before increasing traffic.

Track, once instrumentation is deliberately chosen: visits → tour → price → signup,
first useful task completion, activated organizations, support minutes per account,
repeat questions, churn and gross margin after infrastructure/AI costs. Do not treat
raw traffic or ticket volume as success. No passive-income guarantee is made:
software still needs maintenance, incident handling and occasional support.

## Required before opening sales

Validated payments and cancellation; automatic organization setup and access;
a short in-product start checklist; clear seller/privacy/retention details;
operational backup/restore and incident arrangements; bounded resource and AI costs.
These are separate launch work, not claims that this website update implements them.
